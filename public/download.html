How to install

  1. Tap Download above (or scan the QR code).
  2. Open the downloaded wtt-capture.apk file from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser / files app.
  4. Tap Install, then open WTT Capture.
  5. Sign in with your WTT username and password.

Android only. The app needs an internet connection and opens the live stock system.
